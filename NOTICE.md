# Notice

## What this is

A design demonstration of table ordering, built by O2 Design Studio. It is not Rosebay Home Cooking
& Café's ordering system, the restaurant did not commission it, and nothing ordered here reaches the
restaurant. No payment is real.

## The photographs

58 items, each with a photograph.

- **27 are the restaurant's own** — from its Instagram and Facebook posts, its printed menu as
  photographed by visitors, and its menu card on its Google listing. The restaurant has not given
  written permission for their use here.
- **13 are owned by other people** — Wongnai members, Lemon8 creators and review blogs. Their
  owners' permission has not been obtained. Each says so in its caption.
- **19 are sample images** from free-licence libraries (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, the Unsplash licence
  and the Pexels licence). They do not show the restaurant's food. Each is labelled "Sample image —
  not from the restaurant" on its card and in its details, with its licence, and with its creator
  named where the licence requires credit. Each was cropped, resized and re-encoded for the web with
  its file metadata removed; nothing in any picture was retouched.

No private person is named in the text of this page except creators whose licence asks for credit.
A handle, watermark or signature that a photograph's creator put on it is never cropped out: a
picture that cannot be shown with it whole is not used.

The full record of every photograph — where it came from, who owns it, how it was identified and
what was done to it — is in the studio's private catalogue, and the published menu page at
<https://mpc0367.github.io/rosebay-menu-study/> carries a fuller notice.

## Prices

Historical values read off photographs of the restaurant's own printed menus and, for a few items,
off a drinks poster on the café wall seen in a visitor's photograph. The restaurant has not
confirmed any of them. An item with no figure in the studio's records says so rather than guessing.

## Taking it down

If you are the restaurant, or you own one of these photographs, and you want it removed, contact
O2 Design Studio through <https://o2-designstudio.com/>. It comes down on request. No reason is
needed and none will be asked for.
